Land records you can search, and trust.

Citizens verify their identity once, then search land records. Sub-Registrars check each KYC request for their area and add sale deeds to the database, so every record starts from a real document.

Create accountLog in

For citizens

Create an account, submit your Aadhaar details and photos for a Sub-Registrar to verify, then search records by Aadhaar, property ID, electricity bill number, property tax number, owner or survey number.

For Sub-Registrars

Log in with your official account. Approve or reject KYC requests from your PIN-code area, and add sale deeds to the land records database. Accounts are issued by the department.

Review KYC requests from your area, add a document to the land records, or look up an existing record.
